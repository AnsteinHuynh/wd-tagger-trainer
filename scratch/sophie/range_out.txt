Let me explain caching properly, because once it is clear the three possible designs separate cleanly and you can pick.

Caching means running the teacher over the corpus once, writing each image's output to disk, and then training the student from that file instead of from the images. During the student's training the teacher never runs again. Instead of a forward pass through SigLIP plus a decode of a tar member plus the teacher's adapters, you read 2 KB from a memory-mapped array.
