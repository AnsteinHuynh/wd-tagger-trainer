Multi-depth readout from one pass with `output_hidden_states=True` (`567-593`). → Medium if you concatenate layers
Resolution: DINOv3 needs nothing (`backbones.py:362-394`, `263-265`): no position-table resize, no `interpolate_pos`, no baked-pos cache for `kind=="cls"` — "DINOv3 returns False here (RoPE) … accepts any size". That machinery is for SigLIP2/MetaCLIP2. → Your 512² needs zero position surgery; do not port `baked_pos`
`pos_embed_rescale` (stochastic RoPE coord jitter) is first-class (`train.py:282-287`; `backbones.py:396-402`): "True = keep it ON (positional regularizer); the towers are then stochastic during TRAINING ONLY". → Medium: free regularizer, and your 10k-image regime is where overfitting binds
One grid per batch, always — the 544 class keeps token counts uniform (`data.py:834-836`). → Keep 512² square, never mix shapes
"Fewer, larger forwards always beat chunking." Their Qwen path chunked to dodge quadratic attention (`backbones.py:529-540`: "a 120-image native-512 forward = 123k tokens wedged a 24 GB card"), then found the per-image split was pure waste: chunk 1→60 gave 14 s → 5.5 s/step and 15.24 → 12.86 GB (`MEMORY.md:56-60`). → High: chunk only at a memory wall; you are 11–12 GB into 24 GB
No classification head.
